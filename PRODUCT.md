# Product Context

## Register

product

## Product

Stash is a native iOS SwiftUI app for QR-labeled physical storage containers. It helps a user answer "where did I put this?" by linking physical bins, boxes, totes, and toolboxes to local on-device inventory records.

## Users

- A homeowner or family member organizing storage areas such as basements, attics, closets, garages, and holiday bins.
- A practical user who wants fast lookup, reliable labels, and low maintenance more than social, cloud, or account features.
- A user who may be managing clutter or reviewing unused possessions, so the product should feel useful and calm rather than judgmental.

## Product Principles

- Local first: inventory data should work without a backend, account, or network.
- Physical world first: labels, QR scans, photos, and container names must stay reliable when the user is standing next to a real bin.
- Fast retrieval: primary flows should minimize steps between search or scan and the item or container answer.
- Durable labels: a container's QR identity should remain stable after edits.
- Quiet decluttering: usage and review tools should support decisions without shaming language or noisy gamification.
- Exportable data: local-only information should not be trapped on one phone.

## Tone

Plain, practical, and warm. Prefer concise labels like "Mark opened", "Used today", "Review reminder", and "Nothing here yet." Avoid hype, jokes, emoji, anthropomorphic copy, or guilt-focused hoarding language.

## Anti-References

- Spreadsheet-heavy inventory tools.
- Cloud-account-first storage systems.
- Cartoonish decluttering apps.
- Decorative smart-home marketing screens.
- Loud gamified habit trackers.
